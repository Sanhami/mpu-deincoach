# Brand architecture — final owner decision

## Primary brand

`MPUdeincoach`

The hyphen in `mpu-deincoach.de` is a technical/historical domain attribute, not a separate brand identity.

## Commercial website

- Domain: `mpudeincoach.de`
- Public brand: `MPUdeincoach`
- Function: preparation, Fallanalyse, consultation, coaching, offers, products, MPU-Kompass and commercial conversion

## Knowledge website

- Domain: `mpu-deincoach.de`
- Public display name: `MPUdeincoach Wissen`
- Descriptor: `Das Wissensportal rund um die MPU`
- Function: editorial information, orientation and understandable MPU answers

## Shared identity

Both sites use one logo family, core typography, Ink `#282828`, Signal Yellow `#F8E030`, visual quality standards, author identity and trust language. Purpose—not a second logo or competing brand—differentiates the sites.

`MPU Fachwissensdatenbank` remains an internal/project/source term and is not the primary consumer-facing name.

## Cross-domain language

Knowledge to commercial uses contextual language such as `Persönliche Hilfe bei deinem MPU-Fall`, `Zur Fallanalyse bei MPUdeincoach`, or `Zur individuellen MPU-Vorbereitung`.

Commercial to knowledge should use `MPU Wissen`, `Zum MPUdeincoach Wissensportal`, or `Ausführlich im MPUdeincoach Wissensbereich nachlesen`.
