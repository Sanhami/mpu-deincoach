## Valid approval conditions

An approval is valid only when it records:

1. reviewer's full name, role and relevant qualification;
2. exact scope accepted;
3. exact reviewed commit or immutable review-bundle identifier;
4. sources and versions checked;
5. decision and any mandatory corrections;
6. review date and evidence of confirmation;
7. public naming consent separately from substantive approval.

Multiple required roles must each approve. `APPROVED WITH CHANGES` becomes effective only after the exact changes are applied and rechecked by the reviewer or explicitly accepted through their recorded instructions.
