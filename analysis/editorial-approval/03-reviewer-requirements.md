# Reviewer requirements

No person is assigned by this document. Identity, qualification, scope, and consent must be verified before a name is stored or displayed.

## Role separation

### Editor

Responsible for intent ownership, clarity, structure, source mapping, version history, corrections, and workflow completeness. The editor does not approve legal meaning, toxicology, diagnostic/prognostic claims, or MPU professional standards unless separately qualified and explicitly acting in that role.

### Fachlicher reviewer

Approves only the documented professional scope. Minimum evidence: real identity, relevant qualification and current practice/experience, absence or disclosure of material conflicts, review date, reviewed sections/claims, corrections, decision, and next-review trigger.

### Legal reviewer

Qualified German legal counsel with relevant administrative/driver-licensing law competence. Reviews the accuracy and limits of FeV interpretation, authority procedure, file inspection/transfer, deadlines, report consequences, center changes, cannabis/current-law boundaries, legal pages, and AGB applicability. Editorial disclaimers do not replace legal review.

## Competence by content group

| Content group | Required competence | Routes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MPU procedure and assessment foundations | Practicing traffic psychologist or comparably qualified MPU/fahreignung professional familiar with current Begutachtungsleitlinien and Beurteilungskriterien | `/mpu-ablauf/`, `/mpu-begutachtungsstelle/`, relevant FAQ |
| Alcohol MPU | Traffic psychology with demonstrable alcohol-related MPU assessment expertise and current criteria knowledge | `/alkohol-mpu/`, relevant FAQ |
| Drugs/cannabis MPU | Traffic psychology/fahreignung expertise for drug-related cases plus current cannabis boundary awareness; legal reviewer for legal statements | `/drogen-mpu/`, relevant FAQ |
| Abstinence/CTU | Forensic toxicology, laboratory medicine, or equivalent competence in CTU criteria, sampling, identity controls, hair/urine evidence, and documentation | `/abstinenznachweis/`, evidence sections of alcohol/drugs, relevant FAQ |
| Cost taxonomy | MPU procedure competence for category completeness; source verifier/editor for currency; legal reviewer for regulated fee wording | `/mpu-kosten/`, relevant FAQ |
| Administrative/legal procedure | German legal counsel competent in Fahrerlaubnisrecht/administrative procedure | procedural claims in `/mpu-ablauf/` and `/mpu-begutachtungsstelle/`; cannabis law boundary |

A reviewer must not approve outside their documented scope. One article may therefore need multiple reviewers.
