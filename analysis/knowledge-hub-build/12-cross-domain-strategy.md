# 12 — Cross-Domain Strategy

`mpudeincoach.de` owns consultation, preparation, Fall/analysis, coaching, products, book, MPU-Kompass, offers, local pages, partner network and conversion. `mpu-deincoach.de` owns definitions, broad knowledge, deep educational articles, neutral process, Begutachtungsstellen, authority/report knowledge, glossary and general FAQ.

Shared topics have a service/education split. The hub pages document `ownerDomain`, `competingExistingPage` and `uniqueValue`. CTA URLs live in one typed configuration and appear only after educational content, always labelled as a transition to the other domain.

Current main-site check on 2026-09-02 confirmed a conversion-first homepage targeting “MPU Vorbereitung mit persönlicher Fallanalyse,” with offers, testimonials, local sections and lead forms. This hub targets “MPU Wissen” and contains none of those modules.
