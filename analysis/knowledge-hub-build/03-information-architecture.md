Fifteen pillars are represented in the data model. Only nine have navigable routes in this batch; the other six are labelled as under review without links, preventing empty SEO pages. Historical flat URLs are retained when Phase 5 assigns informational ownership to the knowledge domain.

Primary navigation exposes the most useful P0 knowledge tasks. Breadcrumbs establish hierarchy even when historical routes stay flat. The footer separates knowledge, editorial policy, legal and the one explicit transition to personal help.
