# Legal input checklist

The current Impressum, Datenschutz and AGB routes are visibly marked incomplete and remain `noindex`. Do not treat their placeholders as legal publication text.

## Operator and Impressum

- Exact operator/company name and legal form
- Complete serviceable postal address
- Responsible natural person/management
- Current contact email and telephone where required
- Commercial/cooperative register, register court and number if applicable
- VAT identification or business identification number if applicable
- Supervisory authority, regulated-profession details and professional rules if applicable
- Responsible person under applicable media law if required
- Dispute-resolution and consumer-information obligations, if applicable

## Privacy inventory

- Hosting provider, region and processing agreement
- DNS/CDN and certificate providers
- Lovable and GitHub roles, visibility and processing relevance
- Contact/correction mechanisms and the exact data fields transmitted
- Analytics, tracking, session replay or advertising services (none implemented now; re-audit before launch)
- Cookies, local storage or comparable technologies (none implemented now; re-audit before launch)
- External embeds, fonts, media, forms, email providers and spam protection
- Purposes, legal bases, recipients, retention periods and deletion rules
- International transfers and safeguards
- Data-subject rights, supervisory authority and data-protection contact/DPO if applicable
- Processing records and processor agreements

## AGB decision

Counsel/owner must decide whether this knowledge domain forms contracts or sells services. If it remains a purely informational portal with outbound links, an AGB page may be unnecessary; if any transaction, booking or paid service is introduced, the full commercial flow and terms require review. Remove or replace the placeholder only after this decision.

Record approver, approval date, version and next-review trigger for each legal page. No operator facts have been fabricated.
